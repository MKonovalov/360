| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-30-10-01 | Spoofing | /offerings route access | mitigate | `requireStaffAccess()` as the literal first statement, redundant with the shared `(dashboard)` layout gate — matches signals/page.tsx's belt-and-suspenders precedent exactly, so a future refactor of the layout alone cannot silently open this route |
